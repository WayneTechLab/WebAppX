# Wayne Tech Lab Brand Design Specification

## Protected identity

The approved identity consists of a geometric blue W, a world map, a curved global arc, the WAYNE wordmark, the TECH LAB submark, and an optional service line:

`FinTech | Enterprise I.P. | Worldwide Branding | A.I. Automation`

The source composition is locked. Production variants are created by hiding or showing supplied layers, not by redrawing the symbol or regenerating the typography.

## Official variants

- **Logo:** protected symbol only.
- **Wayne:** protected symbol plus WAYNE.
- **Tech Lab:** protected symbol plus WAYNE and TECH LAB.
- **ALL Text:** complete identity including the service line.

## Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to at least 10% of the visible logo height on all sides. For small digital icons, use the supplied square icon exports rather than compressing the horizontal master.

## Minimum size

- Full service-line version: minimum 1,200 px wide for raster display.
- WAYNE + TECH LAB version: minimum 600 px wide.
- Symbol-only version: minimum 64 px; use 128 px or larger when the map must remain recognizable.

## Backgrounds

Preferred background is pure white. Transparent versions may be placed on quiet, high-contrast backgrounds. Do not place the dark navy artwork over dark photography without a white holding field.

## Prohibited changes

- Do not redraw or regenerate the protected symbol.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, outline, bevel, or add shadows.
- Do not change the world map, arc, proportions, or gradient.
- Do not retype or replace text inside the approved raster logo.
- Do not rearrange the official components outside the supplied platform adaptations.
