# Accessibility

Suggested alternative text:

- Symbol only: `Wayne Tech Lab global W logo`.
- Wordmark: `Wayne Tech Lab`.
- Full mark: `Wayne Tech Lab - FinTech, Enterprise I.P., Worldwide Branding, and A.I. Automation`.

Do not rely on the logo as the only visible company-name reference in critical interfaces. Pair it with accessible text or an `aria-label` when used as a link.
