# Research, IP, and Usage Notice

This package is an original research synthesis and implementation plan for the
SFWA-WTL LAN Builder Designer program. It was prepared from public materials,
open standards, publicly observable builder patterns, and the repository's own
source. It is intended to guide an independent implementation with its own
contracts, visual language, code, and product identity.

- The tracked corpus contains no third-party source code, private APIs, logos,
  icons, screenshots, templates, copied visual assets, or proprietary datasets.
- External names, marks, and URLs are intentionally omitted from the
  distributable catalog. `source_ref` values are local research keys, not
  vendor endpoints or runtime dependencies.
- Standards and third-party libraries retain their own licenses and terms.
- Before commercialization, conduct appropriate trademark, copyright, patent,
  license, privacy, security, and contractual review.
- Do not market SYSTEMX LAN or LAN Builder as a third-party product, a clone,
  or a drop-in binary-compatible implementation.
