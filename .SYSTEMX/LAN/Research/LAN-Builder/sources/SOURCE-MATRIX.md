## Key format

- `REF-001` through `REF-160` identify primary capability records.
- `STD-001` through `STD-020` identify standards/security records.
- `CMP-001` through `CMP-020` identify comparator records.
- `source_ref` is the stable local key used by plans, contracts, and validators.
